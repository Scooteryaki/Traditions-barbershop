Traditions sits on Sanford Ave in the middle of the historic downtown district, in a storefront that has been standing since 1926. The brick walls and high ceilings came with the building, and we left them alone. The shop is a short walk from the shops and restaurants on First Street, so it is easy to make a cut part of an afternoon downtown.

Alongside haircuts, our barbers handle beard trims and shaping, straight razor line-ups, and hot towel shaves. Every cut starts with a conversation about what you want. Bring a photo, bring an idea, or bring nothing at all and your barber will recommend something that suits you.
